# Raptor Labs design brief

**Design read:** operators and partners at mid-size firms evaluating an AI and engineering partner; register is precise, calm, quietly powerful.

**Concept spine:** "From scattered pixels to a working system." Loose glowing voxels assemble into one precise form, echoing the pixel-raptor logo and the promise of turning scattered work into engineered systems.

**Delivery tier:** cinema.

**Locked palette (user's existing brand, overrides the violet ban):** ground #050506, raised #0c0c10, line #1d1d24, ink #f2f3f5, muted #a3a8b0, single accent #a855f7. The violet to magenta gradient lives only in the logo and the film. Defense: these are the brand colors already shipped on raptorlabs.co and baked into the logo.

**Locked type:** Geist (display + body) and Geist Mono (labels, stats, step numbers). Inter, the current coming-soon font, is replaced because the recipe bans it as display; Geist keeps the same neutral engineered tone. The pixel wordmark comes from the logo image.

Animation mode: animated-website

**Journey shape:** single-shot. One 15s Kling 3.0 Pro take (start and end keyframes generated first), cut into four frame-adjacent segments so each chapter owns a stretch of the same move.

**Journey:**
1. Intro (scattered cubes drift in darkness): "Engineers who ship AI, embedded with your team." + Let's talk / See what we do.
2. Why us (cubes begin to flow toward center): "Senior engineers, not slideware." Tags: We work where you work, Humans stay in the loop, No handoffs.
3. Method (cubes converge and lock in): "Scattered work, assembled into systems."
4. Ownership (assembled voxel raptor, slow orbit): "Yours to keep, from day one."

**World grammar:** seamless near-black void, dark smoked glass and machined metal cubes, violet #a855f7 and magenta seam light, low-key rim light, locked exposure, slow constant forward glide, subject center-safe, no text.

**Mobile framing:** subject kept in the center third; 720p mobile encodes; copy sits on a bottom gradient.

**Delivery budget:** desktop clips under 32 MiB total, mobile under 16 MiB total.

**Section plan (after the journey):**
1. What we do: bento (6 cells, cell 1 large with tint, cell 6 wide).
2. How we work: full-bleed photo band + 3-step rail.
3. The terms, in numbers: mono stat strip (commitments, not marketing claims).
4. Where we help: index rows (name left, two-column list right).
5. Founding note: centered manifesto.
6. Contact: split intro + form (stored in D1).
Eyebrows: none.

**Asset plan:** film keyframes, storyboard (refs/), 6-glyph icon sheet sliced, blueprint grid plate, team photo band, cover scene composed into a Raptor Labs OG card, head kit derived from the user's logo.

**CTA inventory:**
- Nav "Let's talk": mono bracket link, brackets spread on hover.
- Hero "Let's talk": solid violet notched block, arrow nudges.
- Hero "See what we do": underlined text link, underline turns violet.
- Contact "Send message": outlined block, violet fill wipes left to right.
- 404 "Back to Raptor Labs" / "Try again": hairline outline, violet border on hover.

**Anti-convergence ledger:** first build in this chat; no previous identity to differ from.
